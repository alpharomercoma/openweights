  1. Ulaanbaatar

    Capital and largest city of Mongolia

    Overview

    Ulaanbaatar is the capital and most populous city of Mongolia. It has a population of 1.67 million, and is the coldest capital city in the world by average yearly temperature. The municipality is located in north central Mongolia at an elevation of about 1,300 metres (4,300 ft) in a valley on the Tuul River and at the southwestern edge of the Khentii Mountains. Khara Tüne of Tuul River(Mongolian: Туулын Хар Түнэ ), the Orda of Toghrul Khan, served as an important alliance centre between the Khamag Mongol Khanate and Keraites during the 12th century.The city was founded in 1639 as a nomadic Buddhist monastic centre, changing location 29 times, and was permanently settled at its modern location in 1778.Wikipedia
